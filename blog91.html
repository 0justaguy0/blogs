To stabilize this current, Lord Shiva assumed the form of Sharabeshwara—an eight-legged feline-avian hybrid.

However, when even the physical restraint of Sharabeshwara proved insufficient to contain Narasimha’s expanding current, the ultimate primordial feminine energy emerged from the third eye of Shiva-Sharabha. This was Sri Maha Pratyangira Devi. Known as the "Mother of the Void" (Shunya), she represents a state of pure unmanifest consciousness where all dualistic concepts of fear, pain, and time are neutralized. Her cosmic form, featuring a male lion’s head on a female body, symbolizes the absolute integration of Purusha and Prakriti.
